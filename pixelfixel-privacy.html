PixelFixel Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Overview

PixelFixel is designed to process images on your device. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information from you.

Photos and Files

When you choose an image, PixelFixel uses that image to create previews and export print-ready files. Images are processed locally on your device. PixelFixel does not upload your images to a server.

If you choose to save an export to your photo library, the app asks iOS for permission to save the file.

Data Collection

PixelFixel does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking.
