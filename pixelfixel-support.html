Common Help

  • Use Fill area to avoid empty margins around the print.
  • Use Fit entire image when you want the whole image visible.
  • For very large prints, lower DPI on iPhone or export on Mac.
  • TIFF is best for maximum quality; JPEG is easier to share.

App Store Information

PixelFixel does not require an account. Images are processed on your device unless you choose to export or share them.
